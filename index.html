Krishnamurti Paddhati

KP Astrology

  • A refined system of Vedic astrology developed by Prof. K.S. Krishnamurti.
  • Divides the zodiac into 249 sub-divisions, each ruled by a star lord and a sub lord.
  • Judges an event through the cusp sub lord of the relevant house.
  • Designed for precise timing, using planetary periods and transits.
  • Needs accurate birth date, birth time and birth place.
  • Suited to focused questions on career, marriage, finance, education and more.

Frequently Asked Questions

What details do I need for a Kundali?

Your full name, date of birth, exact birth time and birth place. Accurate birth time matters most, especially for KP Astrology.

What if I don’t know my exact birth time?

Share whatever you know (an approximate time, family records or a birth certificate) and discuss it with Sunil ji on WhatsApp before booking.

What is the difference between KP and Vedic astrology?

Both come from the Vedic tradition. Vedic Jyotish reads the whole birth chart with its houses, signs, nakshatras and Dasha periods. KP Astrology adds star lords and sub lords, and uses the cusp sub lord to answer specific questions and timing more precisely.
